How it works

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Any travel photo. Wide photos become a square card with the paper below; tall or square photos sit beside the paper.

  2. 2

    Name the place

    Add the location, the month, and up to three words. Leave the words blank and Gemini suggests them from the photo.

  3. 3

    Keep it or send it

    About a minute later the stamp is drawn. Download the postcard, or schedule it to arrive by email on a date you pick.
