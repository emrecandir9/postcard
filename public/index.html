Anatomy of a postcard

Every card is put together the same way, from your photo and a few words.

  1. 1Your photo Cropped to a fixed shape, otherwise untouched.
  2. 2Aged paper Drawn fresh for each card.
  3. 3Rubber stamp A small print of the scene, carved from your photo in 2–4 inks.
  4. 4Location
  5. 5Three words Yours, or suggested from the photo.
  6. 6Month and year

How it works

Three steps, and a short wait while the stamp is carved.

  1. 1

    Upload a photo

    Any travel photo. Wide ones become a square card with the paper below; tall ones sit beside the paper.

  2. 2

    Add a place and a few words

    Type them in, or leave them blank: the place and date come from the photo's own data, and the words are suggested.

  3. 3

    Download it, or schedule it

    Save the postcard, or pick a date and it arrives by email, like a note from the trip.
